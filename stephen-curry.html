About Stephen Curry

Stephen Curry is one of the greatest shooters in basketball history. He is known for his incredible three-point shooting, quick ball handling, and ability to score from almost anywhere on the court.

Quick Facts

  • Born: March 14, 1988
  • Team: Golden State Warriors
  • Position: Point Guard
  • Known for: Three-point shooting
